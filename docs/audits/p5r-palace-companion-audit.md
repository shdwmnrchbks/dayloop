# P5R Palace companion audit

The P5R route is based primarily on Alyookid's *Persona 5 The Royal 100%
Achievements + Perfect Schedule*. Earlier imports preserved the calendar order
but compressed too much Palace guidance into generic infiltration rows. That
made Dayloop a schedule checker rather than a complete companion during the
Palace itself.

This pass introduces an optional `Step.tip` field. Required route objectives
remain individually checkable tasks; contextual combat, farming, and navigation
advice opens through a skin-aware **Tips** action on the relevant row. Tips are
also searchable.

The first-Palace economy is now explicit on April 15: farm ¥15,000 plus the
cost of missing materials, budget ¥2,050 per Tin Clasp and ¥1,150 per Silk Yarn,
and stop before the three-Shadow/treasure-chest story cutoff. The guide's
¥19,100 example is retained for two missing Tin Clasps. April 18 purchases only
the inventory shortfall.

Palace route days from the Castle through the Laboratory now separate captures,
Will Seeds, route gates, puzzle answers, fusion/tool objectives, carry-out
Personas, money/level targets, and boss execution into discrete tasks wherever
the imported row had combined them. Combat explanations move to Tips so the
checklist itself remains readable.

Facts are paraphrased under the repository's source policy; no guide prose or
raw archive is shipped.
